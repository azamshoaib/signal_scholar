Three signals, one transparent score

Every ranked paper shows exactly why it landed where it did — no black box.

🎓

Author reputation

h-index normalized by academic age, so a strong early-career researcher isn't buried under a senior PI who's simply had more career-years to accumulate citations.

📈

Citation velocity

Citations per year since publication, not lifetime total — so a great paper from last year isn't outranked by a mediocre one that's just had a decade to rack up citations.

🎯

Influential-citation ratio

How often a paper is genuinely built on, not just name-dropped in a related-work paragraph — filtering out citations that are passing mentions rather than real influence.
